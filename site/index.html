Questions

Before you ask.

Does it submit my applications for me?

No. Tassel fills in the forms and stops at the review page. You read every application and submit it yourself. It also won't pay fees, create accounts, or tick "I certify" boxes.

Is this allowed?

You are still the one applying. Tassel types into the same form you would, in a browser on your own computer, and you review and submit. Some programs restrict AI-written statements, so treat essay drafts as a starting point and check each program's rules.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your computer. The only thing sent out is what the AI needs to answer a form or draft an essay: your profile and the labels on the page. If you connect your own AI app, that app's privacy terms apply to what it reads.

Do I need to pay for an AI?

You need access to one. If you already use an AI app that supports local connectors, connect it and there is nothing extra to buy. Otherwise you can paste your own API key and pay the provider for what you use.

Will it work on my university's site?

Tassel works on standard web forms, which is what most application portals are built from. Where a site uses an unusual custom widget, Tassel tells you which fields it left so you can fill those by hand. Always read an application through before you submit it.

My computer warns me when I open the installer. Why?

Tassel is new, so Windows and macOS don't recognise it yet. On Windows choose "More info", then "Run anyway". On a Mac, open Tassel once, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose "Open Anyway".
